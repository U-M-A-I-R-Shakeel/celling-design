Craftsman FAQ

Does the craftsman visit before quoting?

Yes, the visit and measuring are free in every Kuwait governorate, at a time that suits you.

Do you supply materials or labour only?

Both: labour only if you have materials, or materials plus labour at a clear price.

Do you take on full villa projects?

Yes, from ceilings and partitions to decor, handed over paint-ready.

How long does a project take?

A normal room ceiling usually takes 2–3 days; a lounge or layered design takes 4–7 days, depending on size and detail.

What do you need to start?

We need photos of the space, rough sizes, an idea of the work and your area. Then we arrange the visit, price and start date.
