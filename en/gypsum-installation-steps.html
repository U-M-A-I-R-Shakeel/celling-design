Gypsum board installation steps in Kuwait: a practical guide

If you want gypsum work that stays neat without cracks or waves later, you need the right steps from the start to the final finish.

1) Before installing: what to decide

  • Type of work: ceiling, partitions or wall decor.
  • Where lights and spotlights go, from the start.
  • Accurate measurements in metres.
  • Whether you need sound insulation or other fittings.

2) Installing the frame (the key stage)

The frame is the foundation. If it's straight and accurate the finish is clean; if not, waves show even after painting.

3) Boards and joints

After fixing the boards, joints are taped and filled, and the compound must dry fully before sanding.

4) Final finishing

A neat finish means clean corners, correct sanding and even filling, then paint.

Common mistakes

  • Sanding before the compound is dry.
  • Leaving electrical planning to the last minute.
  • Not treating corners and edges properly.
