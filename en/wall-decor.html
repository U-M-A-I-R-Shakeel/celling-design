Frequently asked questions

Can you build a design from a photo I send?

Yes. Send the photo and wall size and we'll adapt the design to your space and budget.

Can a gypsum wall hold a large TV?

Yes. We fit timber or steel backing inside the wall where the TV bracket goes, before closing it.

How long does a lounge or room take?

A standard room ceiling usually takes 2–3 days; a lounge or multi-level design takes 4–7 days, depending on size and detail.
