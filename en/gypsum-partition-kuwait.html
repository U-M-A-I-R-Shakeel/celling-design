Gypsum partitions in Kuwait: dividing rooms and offices neatly

When is a partition a great choice?

  • When you want a quick division in a short time.
  • When you expect changes later.
  • When you want a neat look like an original wall.

Common examples

  • Splitting a large room into two kids' rooms or a study.
  • Creating a meeting room inside an office.
  • Dividing reception, a corridor and work rooms.

Sound insulation in partitions

For bedrooms, offices and clinics sound insulation matters. It depends on the partition's thickness, the core and the number of boards.

Tips for a neat partition

  • Decide the door position from the start.
  • Keep corners straight and the finish clean.
  • Don't just pick the cheapest – pick work that lasts.
