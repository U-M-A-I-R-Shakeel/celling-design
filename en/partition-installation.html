Installing gypsum partitions

If you want to divide a room, lounge or office quickly and neatly, gypsum partitions are one of the best options. But a good partition depends on getting the details right before installation.

Before installing: what to decide

  • Where the partition goes, so doors and windows still open freely.
  • Whether you need sound insulation (important for bedrooms and offices).
  • The door position and opening direction, if any.
  • Whether switches or sockets go inside the partition.

Steps to install a partition properly

  1. Mark the line precisely on the floor and ceiling.
  2. Fix the metal frame so the partition is straight and solid.
  3. Fix gypsum boards on both sides.
  4. Add insulation inside if it's for a bedroom, office or clinic.
  5. Tape and fill the joints, then sand and paint.

When do you need sound insulation?

If the two spaces are used at the same time, or in offices and clinics, insulation noticeably reduces sound transfer.

Tips for a partition that looks original

  • Finish corners precisely and paint to match the room.
  • Choose the door position carefully.
  • Check it's straight before the final finish.
