Gypsum ceilings FAQ

What's the difference between a flat and a suspended ceiling?

A flat ceiling sits close to the slab as one surface; a suspended one hangs on a frame to hide wiring and pipes and allow levels and hidden lighting.

Can you add hidden lighting and spotlights?

Yes, we prepare the cove recess and spotlight openings during installation.

How much per metre?

Flat is the most economical; each extra level and cove line adds cost. Standard board is cheaper than moisture-resistant, fire-rated or acoustic board.

How long does installation take?

A normal room ceiling usually takes 2–3 days; a lounge or layered design takes 4–7 days, depending on size and detail.
