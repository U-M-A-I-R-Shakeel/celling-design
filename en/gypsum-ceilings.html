Gypsum board types – cheapest to most expensive

The board type changes the price. Here's a rough order from budget to premium, and when to use each:

1

Standard board (white)

Best for: Dry bedrooms, lounges and corridors.

Cheapest and most common. Not for wet areas.

💰 Budget
2

Moisture-resistant board (green)

Best for: Kitchens, bathrooms and laundry rooms.

A little more than standard; resists swelling and mould.

💰💰 Mid-range
3

Fire-rated board (pink/red)

Best for: Open kitchens, electrical rooms, stairwells and offices.

Slows fire spread; sometimes required in commercial buildings.

💰💰💰 Higher
4

High-density (impact-resistant) board

Best for: Busy corridors, schools and walls that take knocks.

Stronger and heavier than standard; resists dents.

💰💰💰 Higher
5

Acoustic (perforated) board

Best for: Meeting rooms, home cinemas and restaurants.

The most expensive; absorbs echo and improves sound.

💰💰💰💰 Premium

Frequently asked questions

How does a flat ceiling compare in price to a multi-level one?

Flat is the most economical. Each extra level and each hidden light line adds material and time, so multi-level costs noticeably more.

Can a heavy chandelier hang from a gypsum ceiling?

Yes. Heavy chandeliers are anchored to the concrete slab or to a dedicated support frame, not to the board alone.

Does gypsum board handle heat and humidity?

Yes, with the right type. For kitchens and bathrooms we use moisture-resistant (green) boards on rust-proof galvanised frames, with expansion joints in large areas.
