Who is CeilCraft?

CeilCraft Gypsum Board is a business specialising in gypsum work: suspended ceilings, interior partitions and simple or modern wall decor, with an experienced team in Kuwait.

We take small jobs like a single room ceiling or a simple partition, and bigger ones like fitting out a whole flat, villa, clinic or office.

Our aim is neat work the first time, and clear dealings on price and time from the start.
