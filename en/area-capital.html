Gypsum board FAQ – Capital

How much is gypsum board per metre in Capital?

There's no fixed price per square metre. It changes with the job (flat or layered ceiling, partition or decor), the board type and the number of light points. Send photos and sizes on WhatsApp for a quick estimate.

Do you offer a free visit in Capital?

Yes, the visit and measuring are free in every Kuwait governorate, at a time that suits you.

How long does a gypsum ceiling take?

A normal room ceiling usually takes 2–3 days; a lounge or layered design takes 4–7 days, depending on size and detail.

Do you build partitions with sound insulation?

Yes, we add rock wool or other insulation inside the partition to reduce sound between rooms or offices.

Can I send photos on WhatsApp for a price?

Yes. Send clear photos with rough sizes and the type of work, and we'll reply with an estimate, then confirm the price after a visit if needed.
