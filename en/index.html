Common questions about gypsum board

Questions clients often ask about prices and installation:

Roughly how much does gypsum board cost per square foot?

It depends on the job (flat or multi-level ceiling, partitions or decor), the board type and the number of light points. Send photos and sizes on WhatsApp for a quick estimate. There's more detail on our Prices page.

Does gypsum board handle heat and humidity?

Yes, with the right type. For kitchens and bathrooms we use moisture-resistant (green) boards on rust-proof galvanised frames, with expansion joints in large areas.

How long does a lounge or room take?

A standard room ceiling usually takes 2–3 days; a lounge or multi-level design takes 4–7 days, depending on size and detail.

Can gypsum be changed or removed later?

Yes. Gypsum is easy to modify: new light or AC openings can be cut, or a partition removed, without heavy demolition.
