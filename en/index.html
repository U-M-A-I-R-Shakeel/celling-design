Common questions about gypsum board in Kuwait

Questions clients often ask about prices and installation:

Roughly how much is gypsum board per metre in Kuwait?

There's no fixed price per square metre. It changes with the job (flat or layered ceiling, partition or decor), the board type and the number of light points. Send photos and sizes on WhatsApp for a quick estimate.

Does gypsum board handle Kuwait's heat and humidity?

Yes, with the right type. In kitchens and bathrooms we use moisture-resistant boards on rust-proof galvanised frames, with expansion joints in large areas.

How long does a lounge or room take?

A normal room ceiling usually takes 2–3 days; a lounge or layered design takes 4–7 days, depending on size and detail.

Can gypsum be changed or removed later?

Yes, gypsum is easy to modify. New light or AC openings can be cut, or a partition removed, without heavy demolition.
