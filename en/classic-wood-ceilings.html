Frequently asked questions

Is wood affected by humidity or insects?

We use wood treated against moisture and termites, and leave proper ventilation behind it.

Can classic designs include modern lighting?

Yes. We can add hidden cove lighting and spotlights alongside cornices and mouldings.

Roughly how much does gypsum board cost per square metre?

It depends on the job (flat or multi-level ceiling, partitions or decor), the board type and the number of light points. Send photos and sizes on WhatsApp for a quick estimate. There's more detail on our Prices page.
