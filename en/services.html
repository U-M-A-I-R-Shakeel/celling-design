Gypsum services FAQ

Which gypsum services do you offer?

Flat, suspended and layered ceilings, partitions for rooms and offices, wall and TV-wall decor, and a craftsman for complete projects.

Do you cover every governorate?

Yes, all six. If your area isn't listed, just contact us.

How much is gypsum board per metre?

There's no fixed price per square metre. It changes with the job (flat or layered ceiling, partition or decor), the board type and the number of light points. Send photos and sizes on WhatsApp for a quick estimate.

Can you visit or price from photos?

Yes. Send clear photos with rough sizes and the type of work, and we'll reply with an estimate, then confirm the price after a visit if needed.

What do you need to get started?

We need photos of the space, rough sizes, an idea of the work and your area. Then we arrange the visit, price and start date.
