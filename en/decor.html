How we build a decor project

  1. Send us a photo of the wall and a design you like.
  2. We adapt the design to the size and confirm the price.
  3. We plan lights, cables and backing.
  4. We fix the frame and boards and build shelves and niches.
  5. We finish and hand over, ready for paint.

Roughly how much does gypsum decor cost?

It depends on the wall width, number of shelves and niches, amount of detail and lighting.

A simple wall with cove light is the most affordable; designs with lots of detail or extra materials cost more.

Gypsum decor FAQ

Can you build a design from my photo?

Yes. Send the photo and wall size and we'll adapt it to your space and budget.

Can a gypsum wall hold a big TV?

Yes, we fit timber or steel backing where the bracket goes.

Do you do hidden lighting?

We prepare the recess, strip channel and wiring; the strip is fitted with the electrics.

How long does decor take?

A typical TV wall takes 2–4 days depending on detail.
