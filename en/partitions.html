Partition types – cheapest to most expensive

More board layers and insulation mean more strength and a higher price:

1

Single board each side

Best for: Simple dividers and storage rooms.

Cheapest and quickest; limited sound insulation.

💰 Budget
2

Double board each side

Best for: Bedrooms and offices.

Stronger, quieter, and can hold shelves and a TV.

💰💰 Mid-range
3

With rock-wool insulation

Best for: Meeting rooms, clinics and kids' rooms.

Excellent sound and heat insulation.

💰💰💰 Higher
4

With glass sections

Best for: Modern offices and reception areas.

Most expensive because of glass and frames, but lets light in.

Frequently asked questions

Is a gypsum partition as strong as brick?

For everyday use, yes, especially with double boards. For heavy items we add reinforcement inside the wall in advance.

How long does a partition take?

A typical room partition takes 1–2 days; office projects depend on the number of rooms.

Can gypsum be changed or removed later?

Yes. Gypsum is easy to modify: new light or AC openings can be cut, or a partition removed, without heavy demolition.
