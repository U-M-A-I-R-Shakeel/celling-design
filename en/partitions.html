What is a gypsum 'partition'? Is it different?

In Kuwait many people say 'partition' to mean a gypsum wall: a light wall of gypsum boards on a metal frame that divides a space without blockwork.

Whether you ask for gypsum partitions or a 'partition', we build the same thing with the right insulation and finish for an office, clinic, flat or villa.

How we build a partition

  1. You contact us with photos or a video and your idea.
  2. We suggest the layout and confirm the price and timing.
  3. For larger jobs we visit to confirm sizes and doors.
  4. We fix the frame, run electrics, then boards, jointing and sanding.
  5. We check it's straight and hand over a clean space.

How much do partitions cost per metre?

It depends on the length and height, the number of partitions, board type and layers, and whether there's sound insulation or decorative detail.

We can usually estimate from clear photos and some sizes, then confirm after a visit if needed.

Partitions FAQ

Is a gypsum partition as strong as brick?

For everyday use, yes – especially with double boards – and we add backing where shelves or a TV go.

Can you add a door or glass?

Yes, we prepare the door opening or glass sections while fixing the frame.

Do you do sound insulation?

Yes, we add rock wool or other insulation inside the partition to reduce sound between rooms or offices.

How long does it take?

A normal room partition takes 1–2 days; offices depend on the number of rooms.

Can it be removed later?

Yes, gypsum is easy to modify. New light or AC openings can be cut, or a partition removed, without heavy demolition.
