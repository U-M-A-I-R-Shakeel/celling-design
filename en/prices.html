What makes up the price per metre?

The price depends on several factors, so we need the type of work and the size before giving an exact figure. The main ones:

📏

Area in square metres

The size of the room or length of partitions sets how much board and frame is needed.

🧱

Type of work

Ceiling, partitions or a TV wall – each is built differently.

🎨

Design: simple or layered

A simple ceiling costs less than multi-level designs with hidden lighting.

🔩

Materials & frames

Standard, moisture- or fire-resistant boards, frame spacing and hanging method.

💡

Light & AC openings

The number of spotlights and the AC and duct positions.

📍

Governorate & location

Location, floor and ceiling height affect transport and time.

Examples of how we price

These show how we think about price; they are not fixed quotes:

  • Simple lounge ceiling: price per metre including the frame, plus the number of light points.
  • Room or office partition: length × height, plus any door or sound insulation.
  • TV wall with shelves and niches: by design, number of shelves and wall length.
  • Full flat or villa: ceilings, partitions and decor in one quote, usually better value.

For a real price we need some photos and rough sizes, plus your governorate and area.

Gypsum prices FAQ

Is there a fixed price per metre?

There's no fixed price per square metre. It changes with the job (flat or layered ceiling, partition or decor), the board type and the number of light points. Send photos and sizes on WhatsApp for a quick estimate.

Which boards are cheaper and which cost more?

Standard white board is cheapest and suits dry rooms; then moisture-resistant (green) for kitchens and bathrooms; then fire-rated; the most expensive is perforated acoustic board for halls and offices.

Which ceiling styles cost least?

Cheapest are 60×60 grid tiles for offices, then a flat ceiling, then one level with cove light. Multi-level, classic and wooden ceilings cost the most.

Is the visit free?

Yes, the visit and measuring are free in every Kuwait governorate, at a time that suits you.

Does the price include paint?

We usually hand over paint-ready; painting can be added to the quote on request.
