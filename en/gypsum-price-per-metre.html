What raises or lowers the price per metre?

  • Type of work: ceiling, partition or wall feature.
  • Design complexity: one level costs less than many levels and corners.
  • Lighting: spotlights, hidden cove light and electrical preparation.
  • Board type: standard is cheaper than moisture-resistant, fire-rated or acoustic.
  • Finishing: jointing, sanding and paint decide the final look.

How to get an accurate price quickly

  • Whether you want hidden lighting or spotlights.

Does the price differ for a flat, villa or office?

Yes, because sizes, design and fittings differ. Tell us the type of place and its size so the estimate is realistic.
