What makes good gypsum work?

When comparing work, look for:

  • Straight ceilings and walls with no waves at the corners.
  • Correct frame spacing and firm fixing.
  • Neat, solid openings for lights and AC.
  • Smooth taped joints that don't crack later.
  • A clean site when the job is done.
